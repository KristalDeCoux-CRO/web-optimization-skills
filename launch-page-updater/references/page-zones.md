# Page zones and launch decision guide

## Zones

| Zone | Sections | What triggers a change | Update frequency | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evergreen core | Hero headline, subheadline, primary value proposition, main pillars | A rebrand, a significant category shift or an acquisition. Not individual launches. | Annually or less | Brand and SEO |
| Proof layer | Customer logo bar, proof stats, customer stories, analyst recognition | A new analyst report, an approved customer logo, updated stats | Quarterly | Product marketing and SEO |
| Capability grid | Product or solution cards, capability sections, feature highlights | A new product or major capability; a capability renamed or expanded | Per launch | Product marketing |
| Dynamic zone | Announcement bar, "What's new" module, latest resources, event promotions | Any launch, event, report or newsworthy update | Monthly or as needed | Content and product marketing |
| FAQ block | Question-and-answer pairs | New buyer questions from a launch, competitive shifts, common sales or support questions | Quarterly | SEO and content |
| Internal links | Links in body copy, related solutions, navigation | A new page to link to; a page retired or its URL changed | Per publish | SEO |

## Decision guide

| Launch type | Announcement bar | Capability grid | Proof layer | FAQ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New product (generally available) | Update | Add card | If metrics are available | Add 2–3 Q&As | Update meta title and description if the product is the page's main focus. Add to the homepage capability grid. |
| Major new capability | Update | Update or add | No change | Add 1–2 Q&As | Update the capability section for that solution area; link from the platform page. |
| Minor feature or enhancement | Optional | No change | No change | Only if it changes buyer questions | Don't touch the hero or value proposition. Use a blog post and a link from the relevant section. |
| New analyst report | Update | No change | Update recognition | No change | Update the relevant solution page and the homepage. |
| New customer or case study | No change | No change | Add to logos and stories | No change | If the carousel is full, replace the weakest story. Prefer stories with quantified outcomes. |
| Updated stat or benchmark | No change | No change | Update stat and source | No change | Always update the source citation; stale dates erode trust. |
| New integration or partner | No change | Only for a marquee partner | If it changes the ecosystem count | No change | Update integration counts where they appear. |
| Pricing or packaging change | Only if significant | No change | No change | Update pricing questions | Check meta descriptions for stale pricing language. Keep prices out of the hero. |
| Conference or event | Update | No change | No change | No change | Announcement bar only. Event promotions elsewhere become stale when the event passes. |

## The "What's new" module

A permanent module below the capability sections and above the FAQ, holding two or three cards. Each card has a category label, a headline, one sentence and a "Read more" link. When a new card is added at the top, the oldest card rotates out. It keeps pages current with a content swap instead of a redesign.
